GALAGA · OSCILLOSCOPE

The game is drawn as lines. The picture is also a sound: left channel = X, right channel = Y. Put a real oscilloscope in X/Y mode on the headphone jack and it shows the game.
L (X) in green, R (Y) in pale blue: one video frame of the signal.
Arrow keys or A / D move, Space fires, P pauses.
XY out plays the picture as sound (left = X, right = Y) for a real scope: turn the volume up, use DC coupling, and see the notes. It sounds like a harsh buzz. With XY out off, the page is silent.
